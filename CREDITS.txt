GOLDENEYE 007 PLUS — CREDITS
=============================

GoldenEye 007 Plus exists because of the work of many original developers,
decompilation researchers, preservationists, reverse engineers, emulator and
tool authors, testers, and community contributors.

GOLDENEYE 007 DECOMPILATION
---------------------------
GoldenEye 007 Plus is built on the GoldenEye 007 decompilation project and its
community contributors.

Primary upstream reference supplied for this project:
  https://gitlab.com/kholdfuzion/goldeneye_src

The decompilation project's matching/reconstruction work is fundamental to
this project. Preserve its authorship history and upstream documentation.

PERFECT DARK DECOMPILATION
--------------------------
Perfect Dark decompilation project and contributors.

Ryan Dwyer is credited in the supplied Perfect Dark repository's MIT license:
  Copyright (c) 2022 Ryan Dwyer

Perfect Dark is used as an engine-lineage research and selective backport
reference. GoldenEye 007 Plus does not treat Perfect Dark as a wholesale
replacement for GoldenEye behavior.

GOLDENEYE 007 PLUS PROJECT
--------------------------
Design, integration, optimization, testing, menu/UI work, co-op expansion,
multiplayer expansion, gameplay options, debugging, preservation decisions,
and cumulative project direction: GoldenEye 007 Plus project contributors.

Development/research assistance has included OpenAI ChatGPT for source
analysis, implementation assistance, build troubleshooting, documentation,
and comparative research.

COMMUNITY / RESEARCH REFERENCES
-------------------------------
Additional community work that has informed research or future compatibility
planning includes, where applicable:

- Goldfinger 64 / SubDrag community research and tooling;
- GoldenEye Setup Editor ecosystem and related GoldenEye/Perfect Dark modding
  research;
- GoldenEye XBLA prototype research used for function/name/behavior comparison;
- TimeSplitters lineage research where symbols or algorithms provide useful
  provenance clues;
- Project64 and GLideN64 for emulator testing and regression investigation.
